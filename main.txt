Introduction
This document describes the approach and methodologies used by the testing team to plan, organize and perform the testing of the ”Buddy Bot” project.

The “Buddy Bot” is a virtual assistant designed to streamline the onboarding process for new employees, customers, and community members through conversational guidance, resource access, FAQs, and human support when necessary.
The project consists of 2 main components:
Admin panel will allow Location Heads, HRs and community drivers to create and maintain tasks and broadcasts of FS Chatbot. 
Telegram bot will allow users to get acquainted with the company in the game form with various tasks typical for employees.
